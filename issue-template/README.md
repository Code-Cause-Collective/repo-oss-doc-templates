# Issue Templates

Issue templates help standardize how contributors report bugs or request features. They ensure that maintainers receive all the necessary information to understand, reproduce, and prioritize issues efficiently. Using templates also reduces back-and-forth communication and speeds up issue resolution.

## Examples

> [!NOTE]
> The examples *we* provided are opinionated and intended as guidance. We encourage you to explore other approaches, adapt them to your workflow, and expand upon them to fit the specific needs of your project.
