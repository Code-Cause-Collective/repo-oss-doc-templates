# Repository OSS Documentation Templates

A collection of opinionated documentation templates for open-source projects, designed to promote clarity and consistency.

---

## Why This Exists

Maintaining high-quality documentation is important for open-source and all kind of projects, but writing it from scratch every time is tedious.  
This repository provides **opinionated templates** to help you get started quickly.

---

## What’s Included

- **README template** – A clear, concise starting point for your project’s main documentation.
- **CONTRIBUTING template** – Guidelines for contributors, including code style, PR workflow, and communication norms.
- **CODE_OF_CONDUCT template** – A standard code of conduct to foster a welcoming and inclusive community.
- **LICENSE template** – A starting point for adding an open-source license.
- **Issue & PR templates** – Structured templates designed to streamline issue reporting and enhance contribution quality.

*All templates are opinionated and meant as guidelines—you’re encouraged to adapt them to fit your project’s needs.*

---

## How to Use

1. Copy the templates you need into your project root.  
2. Customize them to fit your project’s specific goals, style, and community norms.  
3. Commit them to your repository.
